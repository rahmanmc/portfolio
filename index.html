ABOUT ATHAU RAHMAN

Background & Mission

Translating Meaning, Context, and Culture.

My name is Athau Rahman MC, based in Olavattur, Kerala. I am a dedicated student translator undergoing rigorous foundational training in professional translation studies.

I am currently pursuing my translation coursework at Transarabia Institute in Mukkam, specializing in bidirectional translation between English and Arabic.

As an emerging translator, I treat translation not simply as literal word replacement, but as an art of preserving tone, cultural subtlety, and message clarity. I am eager to apply my foundational learning to real-world translation exercises, practice assignments, and future collaborations.
